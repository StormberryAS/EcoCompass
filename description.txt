Eco‑Compass – Solar‑Powered Energy Planner

Core Functionality:
- Estimate daily solar‑energy generation for a given roof orientation and tilt.
- Interactive 3‑D glass‑styled roof model (using CSS 3D transforms).
- Export a PDF report summarizing potential savings and energy yield.

Why it fits:
- Directly utilizes SunApp’s solar‑position data for a practical sustainability use‑case.
- Appeals to eco‑conscious users and homeowners.
- Maintains the high-end, vibecoded aesthetic.

Potential UI Elements:
- 3D roof visualizer that updates as you change orientation.
- Energy generation graph (glass‑morphism style).
- Simple "Download Report" button.

Design & Development Rules:
- All pages must have footers as similar to SunApp as possible.
- The favicon in SVG must be in the mainhead.
- Once ready, I want to see it in localhost before we git push it.
